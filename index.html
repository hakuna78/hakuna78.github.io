Ongoing · since Oct. 2026

Korean audio-visual speech recognition with a multi-modal LLM

Team project · AiEYES, HUFS

Speech recognition breaks down in noisy places; lip movement does not. We adapt Omni-AVSR (ICASSP 2026), a single LLM-based model that handles audio-only, visual-only, and audio-visual recognition, to Korean.

  • Fine-tuning on the AI Hub Korean lip-reading dataset with LoRA.
  • Added an auxiliary phoneme-level CTC loss so audio-visual tokens align with Korean pronunciation.
  • Next: CTC on/off ablation, noise robustness across SNR levels, and jamo vs. syllable targets.

Ongoing · since Dec. 2025

Attention-based spatial estimation of hourly PM10/PM2.5 at unmonitored stations

Undergraduate researcher, Donghyun Lee AI Group · Prof. Donghyun Lee

Estimating fine-dust levels where no sensor exists, using the stations around it. Stations are held out by fold, so the model never sees the place it has to predict.

  • Residual correction on top of an IDW + ordinary kriging baseline.
  • LightGBM on 45 features from ten interpolators (wind-aware IDW, CAMS-based regression kriging) and lagged estimates.
  • A neighbor-attention network: the 24 nearest stations become tokens, self-attention relates them, and a query built from the target station's features picks which neighbors to trust.
  • Averaging both corrections beat either model alone on PM10 and PM2.5.

Cultural Heritage Docent that Refuses False Premises: Graph-RAG + Self-RAG

Team of 4 · BOAZ

BOAZ Mini Project 2026

A docent chatbot for 2,560 National Museum of Korea artifacts. A Neo4j knowledge graph plus ChromaDB vector search feed a LangGraph Self-RAG loop that grades documents, rewrites queries, and checks for hallucination before answering. On a trap-question set: Faithfulness 0.86, Context Recall 0.56 (RAGAS).

HiRAG Legal Chatbot for Korean Labor Law

Team of 4 · BOAZ

BOAZ Mini Project 2026

Hierarchical retrieval over law → article → paragraph → item, with slot-based query decomposition, global re-ranking, and coverage-aware top-k. Recall 25% → 100% and coverage 18% → 86% vs. naive RAG; query decomposition hurt on this data, which we traced to over-fragmentation.
